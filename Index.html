Application guide

How Stale Data Monitoring works

Review records you steward, make the appropriate lifecycle decision, and track assets safely through quarantine and verified purge.

Stale Asset Archival & Purge StandardEffective August 1, 2026 · V2MOM 2.14 Trust & Governance

An asset becomes Stale after 180 continuous days without evidence of access, modification, refresh, processing, or scheduled-job activity. Review it and respond before the deadline shown in the application and your notification.

1

Review your assigned stale assets

You are here because your Salesforce email, or an active teammate’s email, is associated with an asset’s ownership or governance roles.

  • Use Dashboard search and multi-select filters to narrow the table.
  • Open a row to review inactivity, ownership, deadlines, lifecycle history, notifications, and handoffs.
  • Select one or more rows to expose bulk actions and CSV export.
2

Use My Team to share access

Create a team and add trusted colleagues using their @salesforce.com email addresses. Every active member can access records assigned to every other member and can manage team membership.

  • A person can belong to one active team.
  • Removing a member immediately removes shared access but preserves their direct access.
  • Administrators can review, manage, deactivate, and reactivate every team.
3

Choose the appropriate action

Self Purge
Commit to deleting the asset yourself in Snowflake. Notifications, reminders, and partner handoffs stop while a later complete import verifies its absence.
Confirm
Confirm the asset is no longer required. It enters the platform quarantine-handoff process.
Export
Download all available details for the selected records.
Contest
Keep the asset for a valid business need. Complete the exception application first, then submit an approved, ACTIVE exception ID here.
Restore
Request access restoration while an asset is quarantined and still recoverable.

Once you complete the appropriate action, your owner response is complete unless a later notification requires attention.

4

Follow the lifecycle outcome

Detected→Notified→Contested or Accepted→Quarantined→Purge Eligible→Purged
  • Quarantined means access is restricted, but the asset remains recoverable during the configured quarantine period.
  • A final purge notice is sent 30 days before eligibility.
  • Purged means permanent deletion has been verified; restoration is no longer available.
  • Asset status is the operational condition; lifecycle status drives reminders, notifications, and handoffs.

Exception process

Contest stale designation

Before you can contest
  1. Open the exception request form and complete its four steps: requestor, exception details, risk assessment, and review.
  2. Submit the request and copy the generated ID in the format EDG-EXC-YYYY-NNN.
  3. Wait for the exception to be approved and reach ACTIVE status in the exception database.
  4. Return here and enter that ID. The application validates the ID and ACTIVE status before recording the contest.
  5. Exception details are reconciled automatically; no second submission is required here.
